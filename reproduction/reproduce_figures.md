See ../docs/FIGURE_REPRODUCTION_MAP.csv. Figures 1–4 are conceptual diagrams generated by the manuscript presentation helper; their PDF sources/snapshots are included. Figures 5–8 use stored canonical cells (not raw traces). The exact shipped PDFs are the reference outputs.

analysis/figures/render_evidence.py is adapted to repository-relative inputs and work/presentation outputs. With Matplotlib installed, a later authorized execution can format the existing cells. No helper execution or image regeneration occurred in this preparation. Font/library differences can change PDF bytes/layout; byte-identical rendering is not guaranteed. Original reviewed captions remain in the manuscript/table snapshots; the generator is a presentation aid, not authority for editing scientific text.
