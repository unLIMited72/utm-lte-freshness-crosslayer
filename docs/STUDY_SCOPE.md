# Study scope

This study establishes behavior in the recorded LTE/EPC simulation, paired freshness effects, RLC waiting effects and background latency costs. Its transportation relevance is remote aircraft-state freshness on shared low-altitude communication infrastructure.

It does not establish operational UTM suitability, collision/separation benefit, safety improvement, standard compliance, an optimal scheduler, a starvation guarantee, 5G/6G behavior or a universal load law. The controlled policy comparison and cross-layer lineage support a plausible waiting interpretation, not causal mediation of all AoI improvement. Passing a PDR margin does not mean no degradation or no cost.
